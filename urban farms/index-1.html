Our poultry farm

Urban Feather Farms

Founded in 2022 · Poultry and fresh eggs

Godasu Praveen is a final-year Computer Science and Engineering student specialising in Artificial Intelligence at Vignan Institute of Engineering and Technology, Hyderabad. Alongside his technical education, he has quietly built something far more hands-on: a working poultry farm raising hundreds of country hens in clean, organised, chemical-free conditions.

Where most of his classmates chased placements in software firms, Praveen found himself drawn to the farm — to the early mornings, the rhythm of feeding and caring for his birds, and the satisfaction of handing a family fresh, honestly raised chicken and eggs. Urban Farms is his answer to a simple question: why should good, clean food be hard to find in the city?

From classroom to coop

An engineer's path into farming

Praveen's academic world is built on algorithms, data and AI — a field that trains you to think in systems, spot inefficiency, and value precision. It's an unlikely background for a poultry farmer, and yet it's exactly what shaped the way Urban Farms is run today. Every part of the farm follows a system: feeding schedules, hygiene routines, record-keeping that would make any engineer proud.
